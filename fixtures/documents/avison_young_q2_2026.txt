OFFLINE FIXTURE - summary (not a copy) of Avison Young, Central London Office Market Analysis, Q2 2026.
Source: https://www.avisonyoung.co.uk/central-london-office-analysis

Take-up
Central London take-up totalled 2.6m sq ft in Q2 2026, 6% above the 10-year average (all quarters) and the largest quarter in a year.

Vacancy
Central London vacancy was unchanged at 6.3%. Midtown vacancy fell 60 basis points to 4.3%, the lowest in London. Grade A vacancy rose 40 basis points to 1.9%; Grade A vacancy was 1.3% in both the West End and the City.

Rents
Central London prime rental growth was 6.5% year on year. Mayfair, St James's and Canary Wharf were among the notable growth markets.

Occupiers
AI-sector take-up was about 500,000 sq ft in Q2 and 700,000 sq ft in H1 2026. Anthropic took 158,000 sq ft. OpenAI pre-let the whole of Jahn Court, King's Cross, due for completion in late 2026. Google's 860,000 sq ft King's Cross HQ has completed.
