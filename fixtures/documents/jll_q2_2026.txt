OFFLINE FIXTURE - summary (not a copy) of JLL, Central London Office Market Dynamics, Q2 2026 (published 4 August 2026).
Source: https://www.jll.com/en-uk/insights/market-dynamics/central-london-office

Take-up
2.6 million sq ft in Q2 2026, up 15% quarter on quarter and above the 10-year average.

Vacancy
Overall vacancy rate: 8.6%.

Prime rents
West End: GBP 190.00 per sq ft. City: GBP 95.00 per sq ft.

Development
Space under construction is at its lowest level since Q3 2021.

Sectors
TMT accounted for 33% of take-up, driven by AI occupiers including Anthropic, OpenAI and PromethiusAI.
