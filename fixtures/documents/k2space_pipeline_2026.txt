OFFLINE FIXTURE - summary (not a copy) of K2 Space, London Office Market Review 2026 (updated August 2026), which cites Savills.
Source: https://k2space.co.uk/knowledge/london-office-market

Pipeline
About 8.5 million sq ft of completions is forecast across central London in 2026, of which around two-thirds is already pre-let (Savills).
The City accounts for 4.85 million sq ft of 2026 completions; 37% of the remaining City pipeline is pre-let, rising to 53% in the City Core (Savills).
West End completions are forecast to fall to just 1.6 million sq ft by 2028 (Savills).
